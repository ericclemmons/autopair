## Design Context

### Users

People sharing Apple Bluetooth peripherals between two Macs at a desk, including a
personal and managed work Mac on home or office networks. Setup should be occasional;
daily cable moves should require no interaction.

### Brand Personality

Native, quiet, trustworthy.

### Aesthetic Direction

Match familiar macOS menu-bar utilities and Bluetooth controls. Use AppKit controls,
system typography, standard spacing, and direct labels rather than a branded window.

### Design Principles

- Keep Bluetooth devices, trusted computers, and ownership triggers conceptually distinct.
- Explain security through concrete state: trusted, unpaired, code expired, or unreachable.
- Prefer automatic daily behavior while keeping setup visible and reversible.
- Use progressive disclosure through native submenus and short system alerts.
